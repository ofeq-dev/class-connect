Use case 1: Take bike
Actor: student
The student wants a bike. He goes to the station. The system shows bikes. He takes one. The system records it and the trip begins. He uses the bike for his trip to the library or to the dorms.

Use case 2: Give back bike
Actor: student
The student brings the bike back to a station and the system ends the trip and shows the price.

Use case 3: Pay
Actor: student, bank
The student pays with the bank for the trips of the month.
