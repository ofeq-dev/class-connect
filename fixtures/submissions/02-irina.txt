The actors in the bike-share system are the Student who rents bikes, the Maintenance Team who fixes them, and the Payment Gateway, which is an external system that takes card payments.

Use case: Rent a bike (Student)
1. Student scans the QR code on a bike with the app
2. System checks that the Student has no open rental
3. System unlocks the bike
4. Student rides away and the system starts timing the trip
Alternative: 2a. Student already has a bike out, so the system refuses and shows the open rental. 3a. The lock does not open, so the system marks the bike faulty and asks the Student to pick another one.

Use case: Pay for a trip (Student). When the bike is locked again the system works out the price and charges it through the Payment Gateway.

Use case: Fix a bike (Maintenance Team). The team member opens the list of faulty bikes, repairs one and marks it available again.
