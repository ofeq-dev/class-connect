Bike system use cases

Login - the user logs in with username and password and clicks the green Login button. If the password is wrong a red message appears.

Bike management - the admin can add bikes, delete bikes and edit bikes in the database. The database stores all the bikes and the users.

Renting - the user goes to the Rent screen, chooses a station from the dropdown, chooses a bike and presses Rent. Then the bike is unlocked. Then they ride. Then they come back and press Return on the screen and pay.

Actors: user, admin, database.
